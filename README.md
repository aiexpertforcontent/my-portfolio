# Bombay Clinic (बॉम्बे क्लिनिक) — Official Website

Official responsive, conversion-focused website for **Bombay Clinic**, located at Shimpoli Road, Borivali West, Mumbai.

---

## 🎨 60-30-10 UI/UX Implementation

- **60% Dominant Base**:
  - Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) background and Soft Off-White (`#F8F9FA`) for alternating cards and surfaces.
- **30% Structure & Depth**:
  - Deep Walnut / Espresso (`#2C1810`) for sticky header, footer, and high-impact hero headings.
  - Warm Teak / Caramel Brown (`#8D5B36`) for secondary card borders, doctor profile tags, and subtle dividers.
  - Slate / Navy Blue (`#1A365D`) for medical trust badges and secondary accents.
- **10% Action & Accents**:
  - Crimson Medical Red (`#D32F2F`, Hover `#B71C1C`) for "Book Appointment" and "Call Now" primary CTAs.
  - Warm Mustard / Marigold (`#E5A924`) for star ratings, badges ("Verified Patient"), and review highlights.
